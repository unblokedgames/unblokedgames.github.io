Privacy Policy

This page explains what information may be collected when you visit Unbloked Games and how it may be used.

Last updated: October 25, 2026

1. About This Policy

Unbloked Games ("we," "our," or "the website") provides access to a collection of browser-based games. This Privacy Policy describes how information may be handled when you visit or interact with our website.

2. Information We May Collect

We do not ask visitors to create an account to browse our game collection. Technical information may be processed automatically, including browser type, device information, approximate location based on IP address, pages viewed, referral source, and general usage data. If you contact us, we may receive the information included in your message.

3. Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to understand website traffic, improve functionality, measure performance, and support advertising. Analytics services may process information about how visitors use the website. You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Some pages may display advertisements or content provided by third parties. These providers may use cookies, web beacons, or similar technologies to measure ad performance and, where permitted, show relevant advertisements. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policies.

If advertising services are enabled, their scripts may collect or process technical data. The information handled depends on the advertising provider and the services in use.

5. Games and External Content

Some games or embedded content may be hosted or supplied by third parties. When you interact with that content, those providers may collect information under their own policies. We do not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or game providers.

6. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used to operate and maintain the website, understand traffic and performance, troubleshoot technical issues, improve navigation, respond to messages, prevent abuse, and support advertising where applicable.

7. Data Retention and Security

Retention periods depend on the service processing the information and the reason it was collected. We take reasonable steps to maintain website security, but no internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly request personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information may contact us so we can review the concern.

9. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information. You can also control cookies through your browser and choose not to provide personal information in a message.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised last-updated date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, visit our Contact Us page or email us at info.jamesborders@gmail.com.
